## Assumptions and Evidence Rules

- "Last 90 days" is treated as the inclusive 90-calendar-day window ending 2026-07-16.
- Direct-publisher rows use the linked public announcement. Google News rows preserve announcement metadata indexed by the exact linked RSS search. Names are retained as printed; a missing field is `UNVERIFIED`, never inferred from another profile.
- USD amounts remain in USD. They are not converted to INR because no exchange-rate date was requested.
- Every savings band in the playbook is an `ESTIMATE`, not a benchmark or promise. It assumes a 10-30-person startup and must be replaced by invoice, contract, usage, and seat evidence during an audit.
- Vendor pages were checked on 2026-07-16. Pricing, taxes, FX, product names, and account navigation can change; the operator must re-check the live account before advising a client.
- The cancellation paths describe self-serve accounts. Reseller, marketplace, negotiated, and enterprise contracts may require a different path.

## How to Use This Handbook

- `Source recorded 2026-07-16` means the official page below was identified on the research date. A displayed regional price, tax treatment, negotiated term, or live click path marked `UNVERIFIED` must be checked in the client's account.

## Cross-Cutting Chapter 1 - Cloud Quick Wins

1. **Inventory:** Export ninety days of cost by account, project/subscription, service, SKU, region, tag, and owner. Reconcile the export to invoices before estimating.
2. **Orphans:** Find unattached disks, static IPs, snapshots, load balancers, databases, buckets, preview environments, old clusters, and log archives. Require owner and rollback evidence before deletion.
3. **Right-size:** Use p50, p95, and peak CPU, memory, storage, IOPS, and connection evidence. A low average alone is not enough.
4. **Commitments:** Buy savings plans, committed use, or reservations only against the stable floor after rightsizing. Show break-even, term, scope, payment timing, and exit risk.
5. **Egress and observability:** Rank inter-region, internet, NAT, CDN, log ingestion, metrics, traces, and retention. Fix architecture only after an engineer validates reliability impact.
6. **Estimate:** `ESTIMATE annual saving = verified trailing annual spend × evidenced reduction percentage`. Keep credits, tax, FX, and one-off migration work separate.

## Cross-Cutting Chapter 2 - Duplicate-Category Matrix

| Need | Common overlap | Evidence before keeping both |
|---|---|---|
| Chat and meetings | Slack, Google Meet, Zoom, Teams | Active hosts, external-call need, retention and compliance owner |
| Docs and planning | Notion, Google Docs, Confluence, Linear, Jira | Last edits, workflow dependency, guest requirement, migration cost |
| Design | Figma, Canva, Adobe CC, Framer | File ownership, edit frequency, print/video need, handoff dependency |
| CRM and support | HubSpot, Zoho, Freshworks | Active seats, contact/ticket volume, automation and integration owner |
| Analytics | Mixpanel, product warehouse, GA, Sentry, Datadog | Decision tied to each event/log, retention need, engineering owner |
| AI coding | Cursor, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT | Weekly activity, repository contribution, approved data boundary |
| Messaging | Twilio, MSG91, email vendors | Delivery, route, DLT, failover requirement, effective delivered cost |
| Hosting | AWS, GCP, Azure, Vercel, Render, Railway, DigitalOcean | Production dependency, utilization, egress, support and migration risk |

Do not remove overlap merely because two tools share a label. The kill candidate needs duplicated outcome, low use, safe migration, and an accountable owner.

## Cross-Cutting Chapter 3 - UPI AutoPay and Card Mandates

- Export bank and card statements, UPI AutoPay mandates, standing instructions, virtual cards, app-store subscriptions, and gateway dashboards. Group by merchant descriptor, amount, cadence, and payment rail.
- A descriptor is not proof of the underlying contract. Match it to invoice, owner, account email, renewal term, and cancellation evidence.
- Flag price drift, duplicate mandates, failed-but-retried debits, annual renewals, former-employee cards, trial conversions, and subscriptions cancelled in-app but still billed.
- RBI/NPCI mandate thresholds and authentication rules are `UNVERIFIED` in this pack. Do not state a threshold or dispute right without a current official circular linked to the finding.
- Action sequence: cancel with merchant, capture confirmation, revoke mandate if appropriate, monitor the next cycle, and escalate a disputed debit through the issuer's official process. Never request a password, OTP, PIN, or CVV.

## Cross-Cutting Chapter 4 - AI-Tool Overlap

1. Separate **seat plans** from **API consumption**. A ChatGPT, Cursor, or Copilot seat does not prove the API is redundant, and the reverse is also true.
2. Attribute seats by active user and APIs by project, key, model, endpoint, environment, tokens, cache, retries, and business workflow.
3. Test four levers: remove dormant seats; route simple tasks to an appropriate lower-cost model; shorten or cache repeated context; cap retries and outputs.
4. Check security and data-processing terms before consolidation. Cost alone cannot justify sending source code or client data to a different provider.
5. Savings formula: `ESTIMATE = removed-seat annual cost + (verified baseline API cost − measured target API cost)`. Do not count an unmeasured model switch.

## Cross-Cutting Chapter 5 - Writing the Kill-List

Each finding must use this structure:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Item | Vendor, account/workspace, plan, owner, payment rail |
| Evidence | Invoice/statement date, exact charge, seat or usage export, contract term, screenshot or source link |
| Annual cost | Verified recurring amount × verified cadence; separate tax, FX, credits, and one-offs |
| Finding | One factual sentence: unused, duplicated, oversized, wrong tier, avoidable fee, or unowned renewal |
| Action | Keep, remove seat, downgrade, consolidate, resize, negotiate, cancel, or investigate |
| Savings | `VERIFIED` only after the new bill or stopped debit; before then label `ESTIMATE` with formula |
| Risk and rollback | Dependency, data export, migration, service impact, and reversal path |
| Owner and deadline | Named client approver and a specific date |
| 30-day proof | New invoice, credit, cancellation confirmation, usage reduction, or absence of the next debit |

Rank by verified annual cost, confidence, effort, and operational risk. Never call a recommendation a saving until the 30-day evidence confirms it.

## 01. "I don't want to share bank or card statements."

You can redact anything unrelated before sharing. Statements are processed request-time and not retained, and the report uses PII-redacted exports. We need merchant, amount, date, and cadence evidence - never passwords, OTPs, PINs, or CVVs. If that still feels too broad, start with invoices and a redacted recurring-charge export.

## 02. "I won't give admin access to our tools."

That is reasonable. Any access is viewer-only; we do not change, downgrade, or cancel accounts. You can instead provide redacted billing, seat, and usage exports. We never request passwords, OTPs, or CVVs. The founder approves every action after the evidence-backed report.

If the Founder Spend Audit does not find ≥₹50K/year cuttable, the client pays nothing under the stated guarantee.

## 04. "This sounds like generic cost cutting."

The output is line-by-line: item, evidence, verified annual cost, proposed action, owner, deadline, risk, and 30-day proof. We do not invent benchmarks or claim savings before the bill changes. Recommendations stay with the founder; Vognary does not cancel tools or change the operating strategy.

## 05. "What happens to our data?"

Statements are processed request-time and not retained. You may redact anything, access is viewer-only where needed, and deliverables use PII-redacted exports. We never ask for passwords, OTPs, or CVVs. A mutual NDA is available on request before material is reviewed.

## 06. "Our stack is too small to have waste."

That may be true. The ₹4,999 audit is designed to make that test low-risk: seven-day delivery, and if it does not find ≥₹50K/year cuttable, you pay nothing. The conclusion can be "keep everything" where the evidence supports it; we do not force a kill-list.

The point is not austerity. It is establishing ownership and a recurring-spend baseline before hiring, cloud use, and vendor count expand. The report ranks high-confidence cuts separately from strategic tools, and no action occurs without the founder's approval.

## 08. "Will this disrupt production or the team?"

Not during the audit. Access is viewer-only, and every proposed change includes dependency, risk, rollback, owner, and deadline. Cloud, security, data, and workflow changes require the client's technical owner to approve and execute them. No specific saving is promised. Any pre-audit range is an `ESTIMATE` with assumptions.
