# Vognary Validation Playbook

## Primary Hypothesis

Founders, AI builders, freelancers, and small teams have enough scattered recurring spend that they will upload a statement or connect read-only data sources to find waste.

## First Customer Segment

- AI builders paying for OpenAI, Claude, Cursor, GitHub, Vercel, Render, AWS, and domains.
- Freelancers with several SaaS tools and personal subscriptions.
- Startup founders with small teams and no finance ops.
- Modern households with multiple cards, UPI mandates, Apple, Google Play, SIPs, EMIs, and insurance.

## Evidence To Collect

For each assisted or self-audit research session, record:

- Number of recurring items found.
- Monthly recurring spend.
- Annual recurring spend.
- Reviewable monthly spend.
- At least one screenshot or quote from the user.
- Whether the user found one forgotten or avoidable payment.
- Whether the user independently asks for ongoing source refresh (research only; no current monitoring SKU).
- Whether the user would connect Gmail read-only.
- Whether the user would upload another statement next month.

## Interview Script

1. Which tools or services do you pay for every month?
2. Where do those payments happen: card, UPI, Apple, Google, PayPal, bank mandate, or invoice?
3. When was the last time you checked all recurring payments in one place?
4. Have you ever discovered a subscription or mandate late?
5. What would be worth paying for: detection, reminders, cancellation help, usage analysis, team reports, or cloud/SaaS optimization?
6. Would you connect a live source or upload a structured statement export for a private audit where no connector exists?
7. Would you connect Gmail read-only for invoice detection?
8. What would make you not trust this product?
9. If this saved you Rs 10,000/year, what would you pay?
10. Who else should try this?

## Go/No-Go Gates

| Gate | Required Signal |
| --- | --- |
| Interview demand | 100 interviews, 60+ say painful |
| Trust | 30+ users upload statement or agree to Gmail read-only |
| Discovery | 60% find one forgotten or avoidable recurring payment |
| Savings | Median annual savings found above Rs 5,000 |
| Payment | 20 users pay or pre-commit Rs 499+ |
| Retention | 40% return after first audit |
| Virality | 20% share audit report with a friend, founder, or team |
