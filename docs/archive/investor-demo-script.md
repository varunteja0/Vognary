# Investor Demo Script

## One-Line Pitch

Vognary finds and explains every recurring financial commitment before money leaves the account.

## Demo Flow

1. Open the app.
2. Show the empty workspace: Vognary does not pretend it has universal coverage.
3. Connect available sources first, then use a real anonymized structured statement export only where direct access is unavailable.
4. Add one manual commitment such as Apple iCloud or UPI AutoPay.
5. Show monthly recurring spend, annual run-rate, reviewable burn, and renewals in 10 days.
6. Click a high-cost item such as Render or Adobe.
7. Show the evidence trail and confidence score.
8. Change Founder action to Downgrade or Cancel.
9. Export the audit pack.
10. Open `/beta-readiness` and show the honest production path.

## Proof Points

- The audit engine runs today.
- The ingestion API runs today.
- The audit API runs today.
- No server-side storage is required for the stateless audit product.
- The schema and deployment path are ready for persistent accounts.

## What We Are Raising For

- Security-reviewed persistence.
- Gmail OAuth verification.
- PDF parsing quality across real Indian banks/cards.
- Founder/team workflows.
- Account Aggregator and mandate-data partnerships.

## What Not To Claim

- Do not claim universal auto-debit coverage.
- Do not claim regulated bank integration is complete.
- Do not claim financial advice.
- Do not claim stored user data is production-ready until auth, encryption, deletion, and legal review are complete.
