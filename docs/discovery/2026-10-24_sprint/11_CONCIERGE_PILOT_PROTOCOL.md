# Concierge pilot protocol — prepare only

Do not start this until the G1 memo is PASS and the founder has separately closed the production safety blocker. A pass on conversations does not open the public site to real bills. This document does not enable checkout.

## Offer, still a hypothesis

One workspace. One month. At most ten proposals. Four scheduled reviews of 30 minutes. Up to two extra founder hours, matching `src/lib/pilot-offer.ts`. Price hypothesis: ₹14,999 once. If G1 produced a different number the buyer named, use that number only after the founder writes it down. Month two is a new purchase. Nothing renews by itself.

The software is the control desk already in the repo: policy, proposal, human decision, frozen cap, later cited evidence, verdict. No new feature is part of the pilot.

## Who

One approver who passed the interview bar. They define, before day one, the single outcome they want from the month, in their words. Example shape: "I want three upcoming commitments written down before we agree them, and one past overrun explained against a cap." If they cannot name an outcome, do not start.

## Data boundary

Until the safety file is closed:

- Synthetic figures, or a bill the buyer has redacted to merchant, date, currency, and amount, handled in a local or explicitly safe demo workspace.
- No paste into `https://www.vognary.com/start`.
- No bank, Gmail, card, or provider login.
- No Zoho connection unless they already asked and the existing allowlist rules are satisfied under a separate approval. Default is off.

Say this in the kickoff. If they need a live invoice inside production before the blocker is closed, the pilot waits. Do not "just this once."

## Onboarding

1. Kickoff, 45 minutes. Repeat the outcome. Write the policy ceilings they already believe they use. If they have no ceiling, the first decision is to set one. The product must not invent it.
2. Confirm who may approve. If one person proposes and approves, write that on the policy note. Do not pretend there were two people.
3. They submit the first proposal in the product, or they dictate and you type while they watch. They press the decision. You do not approve.
4. Book the four reviews on real dates before any invoice is discussed.
5. Payment, if any, follows whatever legal and Razorpay decision the founder makes later. This protocol does not turn collection on. No payment means the month is unpaid research, and you label it that way.

## During the month

| Week | Founder does | Buyer does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sit through policy and the first decisions. Refuse to guess a missing amount | Propose only commitments that are not yet spent, or label a past one as reconstruction |
| 2 | Review queue. Do not add categories they did not ask for | Bring the next commitment or say there is none |
| 3 | Help cite one redacted or synthetic bill against a frozen cap | Confirm the verdict matches the paper they hold |
| 4 | Close: what changed, what they would not pay for, whether month two is a yes | Answer in their own words. A polite "useful" is not a yes |

Founder time stays inside four reviews plus two hours. If it will not, stop and record the overrun. Do not quietly become their bookkeeper.

## Success, proposed and unproven

All three:

- At least three decisions recorded before the related spend.
- At least one reconciliation with a cited amount and a verdict of matched, within cap, over cap, or cannot evaluate.
- The buyer names one decision they made differently because the record existed.

Miss any one and the month did not prove the job, even if they liked the calls.

## Failure and exit

Stop early if they only want a hard block, if they stop attending, or if the only way to continue is a new integration. Export or delete under the existing privacy controls if a workspace was created. Do not hold a copy of a redacted bill you no longer need.

Refunds follow the published terms if money was taken and the ten-business-day activation rule applies. Do not take money if you already know activation cannot happen.

## Closeout decision

One page: outcome they named, three counts above, hours used, month-two answer, and whether any safety incident occurred. File it next to the G1 memo. If month two is no, do not build the feature they mentioned in passing. If month two is yes, the next decision is still the founder's, and it is not an automatic roadmap.

## Not promised

Automatic enforcement, automatic savings, fraud prevention, a card, a gateway, an agent, or a complete view of company spend.
