1. **Week 1 = Tier 4 (vendor-change seam, 8 seats) + 2 Tier-2 seats** (the sharpest verified evidence sits in finance operations). **Week 2 = Tier 2 completion** (fraud/compliance see WH-2/WH-4). **Weeks 3–4 = Tier 1 + Tier 3 + insiders.** G1 (end of wk2) primarily draws on Tier-4 + Tier-2 bookings against the *vendor-instruction change* decision class.
2. **Booking math:** 2 conversations/day × 5 days = 10 completions/week. At a 25–30% show rate that is ~100–120 outreach approaches. Referrals convert best; open with any warm contact in Tier 1.
3. **Outreach one-liner (Tier 1):** "Research, not a pitch — 30 minutes on what breaks when agents act on payment rails. You're named in the Feb-2026 agentic UPI coverage. Nothing to sell yet."
4. **Outreach one-liner (Tier 2):** "Research question: how do big-ticket authorization decisions actually verify a voice or instruction today? 30 minutes, direct or referral."
5. **Every call closes with two introductions** (last 5 minutes — the scripts file's hard rule). Warm referrals outrank cold outreach every time they materialize.
6. **Log every call same-day** in the tracker (file 01). Names and IP stay private; the evidence rows are the asset.

**Stand-down rule:** if a conversation turns into you pitching, do not count it toward gate math — mark it `FUN` (fund-mode) in the tracker.
