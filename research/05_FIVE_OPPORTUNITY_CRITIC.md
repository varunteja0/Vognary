## 2. The critic's case — why trade-evidence/TReDS would likely fail for a 2-person from-nowhere company

1. **The state is building this exact layer.** TReDS settlement mandate (S.O. 3507(E)), the >₹250cr onboarding mandate, GST e-invoicing, Account Aggregator, GFF-2026 agentic platform launches. The pitch proposes to sell "connectable, verifiable transaction evidence" — the RBI/GSTN/MoMSME complex is already mandating that pipeline into existence. Startups that build inside a state-directed pipe get squeezed between the state's own rails and the licensed incumbents on it.
2. **The buyers we would need are the slowest in finance.** MSMEs: tiny wallets, price-impossible. TReDS platforms (M1 Exchange, RXIL, Invoicemart, C2FO-class): RBI-licensed incumbents with regulatory mandates and their own tech — they buy from startups only after years of traction. Lenders: 12–18-month credit-tech procurement. CPSEs: PSU procurement. Reachable by day-90: none of the four.
3. **The evidence layer attacks the minority of the problem.** Per the ADB survey itself, the gap's binding constraints are USD/local-currency funding, counterparty/country risk, KYC/compliance cost, correspondent-banking limits. Better invoice-evidence connect addresses the information slice, not the funding/risk slice. The $2.5T headline is unmet demand, not addressable revenue.
4. **The trend is closing.** SME rejection fell 45%→41%≈40% for large corporates; the institutional machine (mandates + CGTMSE-class guarantees) is visibly already repairing this market. Entering a market as the state finishes repairing it is the classic late-entry error.
5. **Crowding is densest here.** KredX-class private marketplaces + 4 RBI-licensed TReDS exchanges + Perfios/Karza/AA data-infra + ERP natives (Zoho/Tally) + Big-4 workflow incumbents. A "we connect the evidence" pitch has no verified vacant seat, unlike the vendor-instruction field yesterday (see file 04).
6. **The memo itself concedes it.** Every section of the pitch says "questions to investigate, not claims": no validated buyer, no validated missing component, no evidence that engineering is the binding constraint. It is an honest research plan, not an opportunity. Research plans are cheaper to run when they are 25 phone calls (our instrument), better still that way. The one tool the pitch and our gate share unchanged: talking to seat-holders to find the missing component — the founder's instruction to skip conversations leaves even this plan un-executable by its own logic.

## 3. The steelman — why it might work (kept on the watch-list, not killed)

1. Mandates create sudden compliance demand; CPSEs and >₹250cr corporates must operationalize TReDS flows — a services wedge into invoice-pipeline operations exists, though it's contracting-shaped, not SaaS-shaped.
2. The counterfeit reality problem (deepfake invoices) equally threatens invoice-based credit — the same P2 countersign asset cuts here later.
3. If Stage-1's authorization-proof asset wins adoption, extending the same proof to invoice/receivable provenance for lenders is Stage-4, not Stage-1. The ratchet rule carries: direction stays registered, reviewed monthly (60-min max).

## 4. The decision rules that produce the verdict (reusable)

A direction is primary today only if it passes ALL of:
1. **Named buyer, real loss, personal budget** — identifiable seat-holders who lost money this quarter and sign vendors' payments themselves.
2. **Reachable within days** — the buying decision involves ≤2 organizations and one reachable human.
3. **Verified vacant seam** — competitive scans (files 04) show incumbents stop at a documented border the seam requires crossing; the state is not already building the seam's pipe.

Vendor-instruction verification passes (1)(2)(3-marginal-yet-tested). Trade-evidence passes none of the three *today*.
