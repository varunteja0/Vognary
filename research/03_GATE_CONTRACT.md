## Standing rules (bind all gates)

1. **No building until G3.** No repo spin-up, no code, no landing page beyond the outreach page, no company name shopping. Conversations + evidence only.
2. **Every conversation logged same-day** in the tracker (file 01 §4). Unlogged calls do not count.
3. **Evidence over mood.** Gate math uses scored conversations only, never "it felt good".
4. **Honesty with the pain:** any interviewee's objection that contradicts a wedge is logged verbatim. Objections are inputs to G3 design, not embarrassments.
5. Each pass/fail decision is recorded in a one-page gate memo (what happened, number, action).

---

## G1 — Problem gate (end of Week 2)

**Evidence required:** ≥20 scored conversations targeted by then, of which **≥5 conversations produce Trust-Gap ≥6** (Severe + Recurring + Untrusted) **on the same decision class** — that is, ≥5 people independently mark one specific decision (e.g., "authorize a large transfer on instruction", "let an agent act on a payment rail") as **severe, recurring, and currently untrusted**, at least two of them unprompted-count Severe=3 (quantified loss or real incident with money moved).

- **Pass** → the problem is real and heated; proceed to G2 sprint (wedge selection via Persona reactions).
- **Fail (<5 strong)** → the trust-layer direction is wrong or the audience is wrong. **Action: full stop for 1 week of analysis** — split conversations by persona: if any single persona cohort produced ≥3 strong signals, one (and only one) re-targeted rerun of 10 conversations is allowed toward that cohort's decision class. If no cohort ≥3, **P1+P2 die; move the sprint to the P3 watch-list pool or escalate with the founder.**

---

## G2 — Wedge gate (end of Week 5)

**Evidence required:** of conversations through week 5 (≥25 target),
- **≥3 senior authority roles (Authority ≥3)** independently, without prompting from each other, identify **the SAME wedge** (WH-1, WH-2, or WH-3/4 evidence channel) as the one they would fund or pilot;
- **≥1 written pilot intent** — email or short memo from a seat-holder saying words to the effect of "if this existed we would ['pilot it on X', 'pay ₹Y/year for Z', 'fund it']".

- **Pass** → G3 begins. The wedge with the ≥3 endorsements becomes the one committed wedge. Ties broken to the wedge with the highest summed Trust-Gap among authority people.
- **Fail / wedge split (≥3 endorsement but no single wedge reaching 3)** → the trust layer is a category with no wedge. **Action: do NOT default to "pick any wedge."** One final 10-conversation sharpening sprint may be run **only** if the wedge-split shows a pattern (e.g., banks only endorse WH-2, rails only endorse WH-1 → then the sharing insight is that even the pattern is a finding: the wedges are segments, not competitors — raise this specific evidence to founder). Otherwise escalate to founder for kill/continue decision.
- **All-negative G2** → both wedges fail adoption language; P1+P2 shut. This deposit remains: 30+ logged evidence rows (the first compounding asset, kept regardless).

## G3 — Commitment gate (Week 6)

If G2 passes: founder picks the 1 wedge (the evidence says it; if unsure, the evidence memo decides) and commits to a **90-day build-and-sell plane**:

1. **Written 90-day plan** with one wedge, the top-3 named design-partner conversations from the tracker to convert first, and a kill criterion like: by day 90, **≥2 design partners running a paid or converted live pilot**, or the wedge dies.
2. **Budget line set** (e.g., ≤₹1.5L incremental spend to design-partner support/prototype for the 90 days) — no external raise before G3 completion.
3. **Pilot consent list** from G2 (≥1 written intent → request signature at pilot kickoff; written intent ≠ commitment).
4. **Only after G3**: talk to investors. Raise framing: what 25+ conversations + ≥1 pilot signature proved.

**G3 kill criteria (written before starting):**
- Wedge dies if by day 90 no design partner has piloted for a paid/committed conversion purpose — regardless of enthusiasm expressed mid-course.
- Wedge dies if a partner tests then explicitly refuses conversion — one refusal is data, two independent refusals with the same objection are the kill signature.

## Ratchet rule — the no-reset clause (binds everything)

Wedge hypotheses (WH-1..WH-4) may pivot or die on evidence. The following, once produced, **must carry forward into whatever comes next and never reset to zero**:

1. **The conversation/evidence tracker** (rows, Trust-Gap scores, incidents) — market knowledge asset.
2. **The persona map** of who holds each trust seat in Indian finance — relationship asset.
3. **Any written pilot intent / memo / email** — commitment artifact.
4. **Any shared vocabulary or rubric the industry starts using from our materials** (e.g., the money-moment countersign language) — standards seed.
5. **The incident library** (verified, logged cases of verification failures in money flows) — the proof-of-problem archive.

If the sprint kills a wedge, the next iteration starts from these assets accumulated, not from zero. This is the anti-pivot-loop discipline: **surface features may change; the records/identity/standards/data base only compounds.**
